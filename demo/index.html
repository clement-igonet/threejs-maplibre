threejs-maplibre demos

  • Globe: OSM raster tiles on the WGS84 ellipsoid
  • Planar: flat Web Mercator map, same tile tree
  • Vector: the Louvre from an OSM extract, styled by a MapLibre style (fills, lines, extrusions)
  • Objects: three.js objects placed by latitude and longitude, a 34 m antenna and a marker pin over the Louvre (?model=<glTF url> loads a model instead of the built-in antenna)
  • Bridge: the other direction, the same antenna and pin drawn inside a maplibre-gl map as a custom layer, on its globe (?projection=mercator for the flat map)
  • Bridge, terrain: MapLibre's "3D models on terrain" example ported, two antennas each standing on its own Tyrolean hillside as DEM tiles land
  • Walk: the streets around Gare Saint-Lazare and its hall on foot, buildings and walls solid, a CC0 knight walking, running and idling, keyboard or gamepad
  • Indoor: Gare Saint-Lazare from Simple Indoor Tagging, rooms, corridors and walls on levels -7 to 2, one level at a time or all of them pulled apart (exploded)
  • Bridge, underlay: direction B, a maplibre-gl map under this engine's scene with one controller driving both, MapLibre's labels showing through under the antenna and the pin (?buildings=1 adds this engine's extruded buildings; ?projection=globe puts the scene on MapLibre's sphere under its globe, from 20 000 km too)
  • Bridge, world scale: MapLibre's "Animate a line" ported in world space (?example=triangle for its "custom style layer" triangle over Helsinki, Berlin and Kyiv)
  • Standalone: OpenFreeMap's Liberty style over Paris from one <script>, the built library and nothing else (needs npm run build first)

Append ?tiles=stub to the raster demos for the offline deterministic tile source (used by CI screenshots). The vector and objects demos take ?data=louvre|stub|openfreemap, ?mode=globe|planar and ?style=<url>; every page takes ?lat=&lon=&alt=&heading=&pitch=.
